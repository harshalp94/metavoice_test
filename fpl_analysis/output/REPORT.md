## Caveats

- Rates come from 2025-26; a player's role can change with a new club or manager (`NEW-CLUB` flag).
- Promoted clubs (Coventry, Hull, Ipswich) and new signings have no PL sample: they appear only on the watchlist.
- Clean-sheet odds use last season's defensive quality mapped to the player's *current* club.
- Penalty duty is flagged (`PEN`) but not separately added to the projection, since last season's xG already contains penalty xG.
- Expected minutes come from last season and do not know about preseason pecking-order changes; `GK-RISK` marks keepers not priced as their club's #1.
- Scoring rules assumed unchanged from 2025-26.
